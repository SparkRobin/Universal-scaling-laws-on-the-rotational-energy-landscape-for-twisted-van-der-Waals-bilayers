# Universal scaling laws on the rotational energy landscape for twisted van der Waals bilayers

This folder contains Wolfram Mathematica notebooks for the analytical integration of the interfacial energy density in the following paper and its Supporting Information:

Z. Zhang and S. Zhu, “Universal scaling laws on the rotational energy landscape for twisted van der Waals bilayers,” *Nanoscale* **17**, 8515–8523 (2025). [https://doi.org/10.1039/D4NR04493B](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4NR04493B)

The notebooks document the symbolic derivations of formulas for different flake shapes, stacking configurations, and lattice mismatch conditions. They are research notebooks rather than a software package; intermediate steps and saved outputs are included.
